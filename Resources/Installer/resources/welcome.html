Espalier

Tab groups for any windows, made to work alongside Grid. Drag a window onto the top of another, hold a moment, and let go: they share a strip of tabs. Snap the group with Grid's F5 and F6 and the tabs go with it.

This installer:

  • puts Espalier in your Applications folder, replacing an older copy;
  • if Grid is installed, gives it the same shortcuts as the Mac it was made on (F5: left half, F6: right half, and the rest);
  • opens Espalier, which then asks for Accessibility permission and opens at login from then on.
